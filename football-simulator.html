How this simulator works

This is an arcade-style tactical simulation, not a full physics engine. Ratings and tactics influence generated events; chance quality drives xG; goals, commentary and statistics are based on the same events. Fitness gradually falls, high pressing costs more energy, red cards reduce team strength, and injuries are rare random events.

Sound is generated in your browser. Tap a sound control or Kick off to allow audio. Crowd ambience is an original approximation, not a recording of a real stadium.
